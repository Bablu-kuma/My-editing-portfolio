01 · About me

Editing that respects the viewer's time.

I'm a video editor based in Patna, India, with 6–12 months of hands-on editing experience.

I focus on short-form content, talking-head videos and educational or AI-automation explainers. I edit in CapCut, building each video around a clear hook, steady pacing, dynamic captions, B-roll and sound design so the story stays easy to follow.

I'm early in my editing career and keen to keep improving. I'm open to freelance projects as well as a full-time video editor role with a team that cares about video quality.
